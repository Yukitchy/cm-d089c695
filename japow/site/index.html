FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Support is in English, and we come with you to the counters where Japanese is required — city hall, the bank, the carrier shop. For the doctor there is a medical interpretation app, and if you want to learn, we run Japanese lessons too.

Can you help before I arrive?

Yes. The free orientation is a 30-minute session plus the Japan Life Guidebook, and you can take it before you land so you know the order things happen in.

Where are you based?

Tokyo and Yokohama. Housing support covers the whole of Japan — share houses and rentals nationwide — so you do not have to stay in this area to work with us.

How do I pay?

We will walk you through it when you choose a plan. We also help you set up Wise, which is what most people here use to move money between countries. Ask us on Instagram.

Is the orientation really free?

Yes — 30 minutes, the Japan Life Guidebook and a Q&A, at no cost. Paid plans start at ¥3,000 for customized support, and only if you want them.
